Calculator logic

  • NG uses the supplied 737-800 Category N Carbon QRH table.
  • MAX uses the supplied 737-8 CAT B QRH tables.
  • IAS: table speed = brakes-on IAS − 0.5 × headwind, or + 1.5 × tailwind.
  • GS: wind is ignored and the table is entered at sea level / 15°C.
  • Intermediate values are linearly interpolated only when every surrounding QRH cell exists. No extrapolation is performed.
  • Taxi energy is added using the QRH allowance. Other residual brake energy is not estimated.

NG caution starts at 30.0 M ft-lb/brake. MAX caution starts at 29.9. Fuse plug melt zone starts at 41.0 for both.
